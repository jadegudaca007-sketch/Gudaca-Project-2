Welcome to KCC

A Legacy of Faith & Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served as a home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. For generations, KCC has helped shape graduates with strong academic foundations, solid values, and a heart for service.

Guided by the Gospel, we aim to form individuals who are ready to make a positive difference in their communities.
